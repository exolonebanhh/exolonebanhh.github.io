Overview

Designed and developed a web- and mobile-based rental and reservation management application to automate Mille Billiard's business operations and address issues with recording playing duration. As System Analyst & Lead Developer, I carried the product from process analysis to a working system used by real customers.

Key Highlights

  • Designed an online booking system with real-time table availability checks — reducing walk-in customer queues and the risk of losing customers.
  • Built an automated billing timer that accurately calculates table rental duration and increases payment transparency.
  • Developed digital complaint and feedback modules to organize customer input without disrupting the playing experience.
0% potential duration disputes — the automated billing timer eliminated manual recording errors and made every session transparent to customers and staff.

What This Project Shows

End-to-end ownership: analyzing the operational bottleneck, designing the reservation and billing flow around it, leading the build, and delivering a measurable business outcome.
