Overview

Served as System Analyst for TheSpace, a smart workspace and cafe rental platform — designing a system architecture to digitize workspace/office reservations and food orders previously handled in person or by phone.

Requirements Engineering

  • Prepared a System Requirements Specification (SRS), UML diagrams, and BPMN to define system requirements.

  • Covered food ordering and delivery throughout the building.

System Design

  • Real-time room availability and occupancy monitoring.
  • Payment gateway integration and transaction history records to support centralized reservation and transaction management.
  • Smart Building concepts and transaction workflow automation to reduce the potential for ordering errors.
