Overview

TheSpace rents meeting rooms and offices by the hour, day, week, month, or year — and runs TheSpaceCafe, open to the public, with pickup orders and delivery throughout the building. As System Analyst I authored the full business case and product backlog that defined what the platform would be before development began.

The Business Case

The current process was double friction: employees ordered cafe food by phone — leading to confusion and delays for staff — while customers had to physically visit the office to rent rooms. Three options were analyzed: keep the traditional process, build a booking app, or build an integrated booking + food ordering platform. The third option was recommended — a single app that takes bookings, tracks available and occupied rooms, records every transaction, and lifts cafe income at the same time.

Users fill in their organization name, rent time and date, and room type, continue to payment, and the room is ready. Food and drinks are ordered in the same app and delivered straight to the room they are renting.

Product Backlog — 5 Roles

  • Administrator — room check-ins, room status, and end-of-month reservation reports for analysis.
  • User — sign up, log in, rent a meeting room, and order food delivered to the rented room; switch between accounts.
  • Manager — transaction reports in full detail to monitor users, activity, and properly recorded transactions.
  • Cafe cashier — order queue for preparation, order type (pickup/delivery), and daily order reports.
  • Owner — full control of system, employees, and business; problem resolution and continuous service upgrades.

The Numbers

  • Initial setup budget: $235,000 — real estate ($100k), renovation & furnishing ($50k), technology setup ($30k), kitchen & cafe equipment ($25k), legal, marketing, and licences.
  • Projected monthly revenue: $20,750 — room rentals ($10,000), five long-term offices ($2,750), and TheSpaceCafe sales ($8,000).
  • Monthly operating expenses: $6,800 (≈ $81,600/year) — utilities, staffing, maintenance, and subscriptions.
  • Timeline: 6-month delivery target, with a minimum useful life of 3 years and a break-even target of 2 years.
$235,000 initial budget, $20,750 projected monthly revenue, 5 roles in the backlog, and a 6-month schedule — the paperwork that made the build possible was its own deliverable.

Work Breakdown Structure

  • Project management — scope, timeline, budget, risks, and stakeholders.
  • Requirements gathering — room booking and food ordering user requirements; system features and functionality.
  • Design — booking UI, ordering UI, and system architecture (backend, database, APIs).
  • Development — booking by duration, pickup & delivery ordering, availability tracking, payment and transaction tracking, user/bookings/orders database.
  • Testing — booking functionality, ordering functionality, payment tracking, and user acceptance testing.

Risks Identified

  • Adoption risk — existing customers may resist migrating from the traditional booking process.
  • Technical risk — double bookings, payment failures, or bookings not going through.
  • Investment risk — significant time and budget spent without a proportional income increase.

Strategic Goals Covered

  • Financial stability & growth — cost management and long-term financial planning.
  • Community engagement & brand building — tenant networking events and market positioning.
  • Operational efficiency — Smart Building technology and IoT-based facility monitoring.
